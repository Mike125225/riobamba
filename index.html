What this trip is

Service where the Movement started

Riobamba, high in Ecuador's Andes, is surrounded by Indigenous farming communities facing malnutrition, limited healthcare access, and no sanitation infrastructure. It's still one of the poorest provinces in Ecuador, and it's the community MEDLIFE was built around.

Over the week you rotate through three kinds of work:

  • Mobile ClinicShadow healthcare professionals across Triage, Education, General Medicine, OB-GYN, Pharmacy, and Dental & Tooth-Brushing stations.
  • Development ProjectBuild something the community asked for: staircases, parks, roads, retention walls, school renovations, a well or water reservoir.
  • Reality TourLearn the historical and economic factors behind what families here face, so the service actually makes sense.
  • 40+ service hoursDocumented hours for your program, your resume, and med-school or professional applications.

Before you go

Travel & packing

Documents

  • Valid passport, good for 6+ months past your return date (7 recommended)
  • No required vaccinations; follow CDC guidance and your own doctor
  • Not a US or Canadian citizen? Check your own visa requirements
  • Carry a copy of your passport, packed separately from the original

  • Closed-toe shoes for clinic and project days

  • Modest, weather-ready clothing for community visits
  • Reusable water bottle, sunscreen, personal meds
  • Small-bill USD cash (Ecuador uses the US dollar)

Questions

FAQ

Do I need medical experience?

No. You shadow and assist licensed healthcare professionals at the clinic stations. It's open to any Sault College student, most often nursing, but not only nursing.

How do I pay for it?

A $200 deposit holds your spot, then the balance is due before the trip. Discounts stack and can knock several hundred dollars off. The chapter also runs fundraisers through the year, and those are easier when you're in the group chat early.

Is it safe?

MEDLIFE runs these trips year-round with local staff, an English-speaking Trip Leader with the group the whole time, airport transfers, and safety equipment provided. Bring questions to the info session.

What if I'm not sure yet?

That's what the list is for. Get on it, come to the info session, meet people who are going, and decide before the February deadline. No payment happens on this page.

Can I bring a friend who isn't at Sault College?

Ask. Put it in the form's last question and the trip director will follow up.
